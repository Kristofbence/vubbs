FAQ

Before you ask.

Will it actually sound like me?

That's the part we care most about. You set your voice once — tone, topics, the accounts you want to sound like — and everything Vubbs generates is written against that profile instead of a generic AI register.

Do I approve posts before they go out?

Your call. Vubbs is built to run unattended, but you'll be able to review a queue before anything publishes — and switch to full autopilot once you trust the output.

Which platforms do you support?

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, and Pinterest. More will follow — early access members get a say in which ones come first.

What do I need to provide?

Your accounts, a short brief on what you want to be known for, and any brand assets you already have — logo, colours, footage. No content calendar, no brief to write per post.

How much does it cost?

Free gets you a post a day on Instagram, with a Vubbs watermark. Pro is $99/month — a carousel and a reel every day, across all five platforms, no watermark. See the full comparison.
